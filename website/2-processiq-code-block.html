The problem

Why process improvement stalls

Most efforts don't fail for lack of ideas. They stall in four predictable places, and ProcessIQ is built to close each one.

  1. Gap 1

    The map stays on the wall

    Workshops produce a diagram, not decisions about what to change.

  2. Gap 2

    Opinions replace evidence

    Nobody measures where the time actually goes, so the loudest view wins.

  3. Gap 3

    Automation comes too soon

    Tools get bought before the process is understood, and they automate the waste.

  4. Gap 4

    Changes fade

    Nobody checks whether the new way of working is still happening after launch.

Inside the app

What it looks like

Map

The whole process on one page

A SIPOC map shows who supplies what, the main steps, and who receives the results.

  • Clear scope. Where the process starts and where it ends.
  • Metrics that define success. Each with a baseline today and a target.
  • Unknowns become questions. Blank values get asked about, never guessed.

Analyze and root cause

Every finding is labeled

ProcessIQ calculates where the time goes and flags likely bottlenecks, handoffs, duplication, and waste. Then a guided 5 Whys finds the cause under the symptom.

  • Fact. Calculated from what you entered.
  • Hypothesis. A possible issue to check first.
  • Question. Information that's missing, so it asks.

Automate

What to automate, and what not to

Four plain questions about each step: is it done the same way each time, does it follow clear rules, is the information digital, and how often does it happen. The answers point to the right kind of fix.

  • Hours at stake, measured step by step.
  • Workflow automation or existing software where the rules are clear.
  • AI where judgment is needed, helping a person rather than replacing them.
  • Poor fits called out before any money is spent.

Prioritize

Decide what to fix first

Each improvement is rated for impact and effort, then ordered into a plan: quick wins first, then major projects.

  • Owners and target dates on every approved action.
  • Measures that matter, from baseline to target.
  • A one-page report ready to share.
