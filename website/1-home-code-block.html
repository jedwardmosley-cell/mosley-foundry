The Leadership Series

A six-session series for the people who lead the work.

For supervisors, frontline managers, and project leads. Whether you lead with a title or only with influence, the problems are the same: expectations aren't clear, hard conversations get put off, and change fades after launch.

  1. Session 01

    Leading Others

    Move from doing the work to multiplying it. Set expectations, delegate with confidence, and keep your relationships with former peers intact.

  2. Session 02

    Knowing Yourself as a Leader

    Take the EQ-i 2.0, a validated emotional intelligence assessment, and see how your reactions in tough moments shape what your team experiences. Then learn to choose your response.

  3. Session 03

    Hard Conversations

    Raise issues while they're still small. Know when a situation calls for coaching, performance management, or escalation.

  4. Session 04

    Driving Accountability

    Turn commitments into named owners and real deadlines. Build follow-through that happens whether or not you're the one checking.

  5. Session 05

    Leading Through Change

    Lead well when the information is incomplete. Sort what's yours to decide from what needs someone else, and move your team from going through the motions to buying in.

  6. Session 06

    Practical AI for Leaders

    Hands-on work with AI on the tasks leaders actually handle: emails, schedules, reports, and meeting notes. Then how to bring your team along.

About

I'm Jason Mosley.

In second grade I taught my classmates cursive, because I'd already learned it at my last school. I got in trouble for it. It wasn't in the lesson plan yet.

They needed it. I had it. So I gave it.

For more than fifteen years I've helped leaders build the skills their promotions assumed they already had. I've led a multidisciplinary team through a full cloud migration while running the people side of the same change, so I teach what I've had to make work myself. Now I work with supervisors, project leads, and managers, and I stay in the room while they use those skills on real work.

My style is structured and direct, with plenty of humor. You'll practice in the room and leave with something you can use right away.
