Both Hands on the Wheel

Getting started. Lead yourself. Develop others. Build a life by design.

1 Sign in 2 minutes

  1. On your phone or computer, go to mosley-foundry.vercel.app
  2. Enter your email and the temporary password your coach sent you.
  3. Pick your own password. Use at least 10 characters.
Your sign-in emailYour coach

Tip: on a phone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen, so the app is one tap away.

2 Your first session About 15 minutes, one time

Pick your pathway: Personal Leadership, Leading Others, or Entrepreneurial Leadership. Then the app walks you through five steps, one screen each.

FORGE who is behind the wheel

  1. Who I am. Finish the sentence: I am ... Say it as true today.
  2. FORGE identity. One I am statement for each letter, about who you are. Tasks come later.
  3. Where I am. Score the five pillars from 1 to 5, then pick one to work on.

Discipline by Design how you drive

  1. My road. Add one routine and one person who will know if you did it.
  2. 30 days. Pick one behavior to practice, two minutes a day.

You can skip any step and come back. The bar at the top of each screen shows what is done and what is next.

3 Each day 2 minutes

  • Open the Today screen.
  • Tap Done, Partial or Not yet for today's practice.
  • If you like, tap whether you showed up as who you are.

A missed day is just information. Log it and keep going.

4 Each week 5 minutes

  • Open the weekly reflection.
  • Answer: did I do what I said I was going to do?
  • Check the commitments you kept, and write one line about next week.

5 Every 90 days A season

  • With a coach, your 30 days are part of a season: three months back to back.
  • On day 30, go deeper on the same pillar or pick another one.
  • After month 3, review the season with your coach: your scores then and now, and who you are.

6 What is private

  • Your coach sees nothing until you turn it on in Sharing.
  • Your private journal can never be shared with anyone.
  • Your employer sees group totals only, such as attendance.

7 If you need help

  • Open Guide in the app for short steps on every screen (on a phone, under More). Forgot your password? Ask your coach.
  • The AI companion can help you think something through. It is an AI and it does not replace your coach.
