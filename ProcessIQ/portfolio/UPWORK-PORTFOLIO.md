**Project title** (pick one; both fit Upwork's 70-character limit and its 40-character guide)

**Your role** *(88 of 100 characters)*

> Product owner and builder: designed the method and workflow, built the app with AI tools

**Project description, long version** *(491 of 600 characters)*

> I designed and built ProcessIQ, a web app that takes a business process from a rough map to an action plan people follow through on. It maps the work as a SIPOC, calculates where time goes, flags bottlenecks and waste, guides a 5 Whys root cause analysis, and scores automation and AI opportunities. Approved fixes become a prioritized plan with owners and measures, exported to my AI adoption tracker. Every finding is labeled fact, hypothesis or question. Shown with fictional sample data.

> A web app I built to take a business process from map to action plan: SIPOC mapping, bottleneck analysis, a guided 5 Whys, an automation scan, and an impact versus effort plan that exports to an adoption tracker. Every finding is labeled fact, hypothesis or question. Fictional sample data.

## Live demo link

Interactive demo with the same sample data: https://claude.ai/artifact/7mmZoj7krSkXxDbp9aM1wd

It is private until you share it. To let clients open it:

1. Open the link while signed in to Claude.
2. Use the page's **Share** menu and turn on a public link.
3. Open the link in a private browser window to confirm it works without your login.
4. Add it to the portfolio item as a link (and optionally mention it in the description).

The demo is separate from your own ProcessIQ. Visitors only change their own browser's copy, it has a Reset button, and it never touches your data.

## Adding the item on Upwork

1. Profile, then **Portfolio**, then **Add**.
2. Paste the title, role and description above.
3. Add the 5 skills.
4. Upload the cover first, then images 1 to 5, then the video.
5. Add the live demo link.
6. Preview, then publish.
